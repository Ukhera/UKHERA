## 📂 Projects

### 📊 Power BI

**[Hotel STR Performance Dashboard](https://github.com/Ukhera/Hotel-STR-Dashboard)**
Benchmarked a hotel against a 6-property competitive set over 184 days of STR data (Occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, MPI/ARI/RGI). Found a volume-driven revenue gap: the hotel priced 7% above market but captured 15% less demand, leaving RevPAR 9% behind. Also identified source-data errors through validation.
`Power BI` `DAX` `Hotel KPIs` `Data Validation`

**[Global & Regional Logistics Performance Dashboard](https://github.com/Ukhera/Power-BI-Project)**
Tracks revenue and container volumes against target across regions, countries and months (Jan–Sep 2024). Revenue finished 29% below target, driven mainly by container volumes running 30% short, with targets met in only 2 of 9 months.
`Power BI` `DAX` `Target vs Actual` `Variance Analysis`
